Idris trained in sound design before moving into interaction design, and
still thinks of the seatbelt chime as the most-heard piece of music in the
world, badly credited. He runs the weekly studio and teaches week 6, on the
sonic register, as the one week where his background is the whole point
rather than incidental to it.

Ask during studio for anything about that week's activity or your work in
progress — questions about the audible side of an alert are exactly what
studio time is for. Email if it can't wait until the next one.
