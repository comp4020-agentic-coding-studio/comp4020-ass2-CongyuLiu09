## Before the studio

No preparation beyond having started your Alert Audit draft (due this
Friday) — bring whatever catalogue of icons you've gathered so far, even
incomplete.

## In the studio

Idris hands out a sheet of dashboard icons stripped of captions and brand
context — some ISO-standard, some manufacturer-specific oddities, mixed in
without saying which is which. Working alone first, everyone writes down
what each icon means and how confident they are. Only after guesses are
locked in does the group compare notes against the real ISO reference
sheet. The point isn't getting them right — it's noticing which icons
almost everyone reads correctly on sight, and which ones only the people
who already own that model car get right, because the shape alone carries
no information without prior exposure.

## Afterwards

Bring the same lens to your own Alert Audit draft: for each icon on your
chosen dashboard, note whether it's legible cold or only legible because you
already know the car. Idris is available this week specifically to workshop
Audit drafts before Friday's deadline — bring a rough draft, not a finished
one.
