## Before the studio

Bring the icon or acronym telltale you worked with in an earlier week — the
idiot-light or shared-vocabulary material both work — and a phone or laptop
with a translation tool you're comfortable using.

## In the studio

Working alone, write the plain-language sentence your telltale is implicitly
asking the driver to understand, as if it were appearing on a
multi-information display instead of as an icon: specific enough that a
first-time driver could act on it correctly, short enough to read at a
glance. Swap with a partner and check each other's sentence against the
icon's actual intended meaning — does it match, overstate the urgency, or
miss a detail the icon carried? Then run your sentence through a translation
tool into a language neither of you speaks fluently, and translate it back.
Compare the round-trip result against your original.

## Afterwards

Write up your sentence, its translation, and the one place the round-trip
changed the meaning — a word that got vaguer, more alarming, or ambiguous in
a new way. Note whether the original icon would have survived the same
round-trip untouched. Bring this pair for the mini knowledge check in the
week 7 deck.
